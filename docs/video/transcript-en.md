# Demo transcript — English adaptation

The source recording is planned in Spanish. This adaptation provides the narrative for English
subtitles and can be refined after recording.

“I want to fund an education benefit in twenty years while protecting that goal if the person
funding it dies earlier. We will build the product and check whether the numbers tell the same
story as the contract.

There are two mutually exclusive benefits: a death benefit during the term and a survival
benefit at maturity. Both contribute to actuarial present value. Our starting case uses age 35,
MXN 1,000,000, twenty years of cover, ten years of premiums, and a 5.5 percent technical rate.
The mortality basis is visible and must be reviewed before professional use.

The net premium follows the equivalence principle: the present value of future premiums equals
the present value of future benefits. Reducing the premium-paying term concentrates funding,
so the annual premium rises. The prospective reserve shows the remaining obligation after
future premiums are deducted; after year ten, there are no more premiums to offset it.

Finally, we check benefit decomposition, equivalence, the initial reserve, and the maturity
reserve. Passing these checks does not certify the assumptions. It shows that the implementation
respects the identities it declares.

This laboratory is a foundation, not a finished product. Change assumptions, run the script,
propose another source, or extend the cash flows. The actuarial discussion stays beside the
open code.”
